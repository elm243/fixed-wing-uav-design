\# Assumption Register



This document records assumptions used during the preliminary design and analysis of the fixed-wing UAV. Assumptions are added when analysis depends on information that has not yet been fully defined or validated. Each assumption should be reviewed and replaced with verified data when available.



\*\*Status Definitions:\*\*  

`Active` — Assumption is currently being used in the design or analysis.  

`Validated` — Assumption has been confirmed by analysis, testing, or reliable data.  

`Updated` — Assumption has been revised and replaced by a newer value or statement.  

`Retired` — Assumption is no longer applicable or is no longer used in the design.
